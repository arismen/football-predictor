Bookmaker odds and value

Enter the decimal odds you are offered. Edge is model probability × odds − 1. Rows turn green when the edge beats your minimum.

Keep this well above 0: the model has estimation error.
Stake is shown as % of bankroll. 25% = quarter Kelly.

Goals use independent Poisson distributions with a small low-score correction (Dixon-Coles style). Corners and cards use Poisson totals, which understate real-world variance a little. Probabilities are estimates, not guarantees. In backtests the ratings were useful for match result and cards, but over/under goals was no better than a naive baseline, and no betting edge was found against bookmaker odds. Compare fair odds with a bookmaker's prices before betting, and only stake what you can afford to lose.
